## Paper Presentations 

In each session room, a presenter laptop (Windows) will be provided. Please **bring your presentation on a USB stick** and upload it to the laptop during the break beforehand, so we can start on time. Please note that presentations must be given from the provided **presenter laptop**, since the sessions will be streamed, and all recording and streaming equipment is set up only for that laptop.  

## Posters 

Poster presentations will take place in the entrance hall as well as on the first floor. Posters will be displayed on poster boards measuring 116 cm x 146 cm (w x h); we therefore recommend **A0 portrait format**. Beyond the size limit, there are no specific formatting requirements – the design is up to the authors.  

## Software Demos 

Software demos will take place in one of the seminar rooms at the same time as the poster presentations. Please **bring your own laptop** for your demonstration. For each demo, we will provide an additional monitor (HDMI) so passers-by can see it more easily. If you are using a **Mac** (or any laptop without a native HDMI port), please make sure to bring the appropriate **adapter**. How you prepare it is entirely up to you – for example, a short slide presentation or simply presenting your tool live.
