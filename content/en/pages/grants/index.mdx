## EURALEX 2026 Grants and Financial Support

Thanks to the support of EURALEX and the A. S. Hornby Educational Trust, EURALEX 2026 is able to offer a limited number of grants to facilitate participation in the congress. These grants are intended to reduce financial barriers and to support broader participation, especially for students, early-career researchers, and delegates with limited institutional funding or from low-income countries.

---

We are pleased to announce the Euralex 2026 **Hornby Trust Bursary** winners, who will each receive a grant covering the full conference registration fee: Abolfazl Alamdar, Emmanuel Sylvain Fomat, Marta Garcia-Casado, Jan Ullrich, Pol Garriga Martínez, Edmond Cane, Tomasz Czyż, Nurfitriah and Urai Salam. 

The winners were selected by Philipp Stöckle (Euralex 2026 Chair), Fabian Fleißner, Wolfgang Koppensteiner (Euralex 2026 Co-Chairs), and Michael Rundell (A. S. Hornby Educational Trust). 

We are also pleased to announce the winners of the **Tanneke Schoonheim Grants** (sponsored by Euralex), who will each receive a grant covering the full conference registration fee: Deepak Solanki, Mariia Kopylova, Li Cui, Ketevani Mchedlishvili, Wanjing Han, Seulki Lee, Silvio Licurgo Pires, and Wang Zongyuan. 

The winners were selected by Philipp Stöckle (Euralex 2026 Chair), Fabian Fleißner, Wolfgang Koppensteiner (Euralex 2026 Co-Chairs), and Iztok Kosem (Euralex President). 

**Congratulations to all the winners!**
